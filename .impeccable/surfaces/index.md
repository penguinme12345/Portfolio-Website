# Portfolio shell

Mode: Experience

Audience: recruiters, hiring managers, collaborators, and engineering peers.

Job: understand Jay's cross-disciplinary strengths quickly, then explore real work through an operating-system metaphor.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A matte desktop workspace turns Jay's portfolio into a calm, direct application browser. It refuses the glossy Vista imitation and the generic scrolling portfolio while retaining the OS interaction model.

OWN-WORLD: Warm gray desktop, white application surfaces, soft gray sidebars, black geometric typography, coral-red active states, thin gray rules, 18px shell corners, 14px media corners, and one grounded down-right shadow.

STORY: About opens automatically and establishes Jay's mechanical engineering and data-analysis focus. Visitors then use persistent navigation, desktop shortcuts, or the dock to open projects, CAD, experience, files, Google Docs-style editing, finance, GitHub, contact, settings, and AI search.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Three large launch icons sit left of a 72/28 split application window. The titlebar centers the current app name. About leads with one wide alpine image, Jay's name and role, two concise information panels, and the supplied summary. A floating dock overlaps the lower edge.

FORM: User-pinned desktop discovery interface based directly on the supplied reference and approved generated comps. Seed key: user-reference-2026-09-13.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every newly generated shipping raster carrying its provenance
